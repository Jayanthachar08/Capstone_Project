💡
Scientific Interpretation of PMRI: PMRI measures reference match quality and cohort density. It is not a failure probability, nor does a high PMRI mean the cell is healthy. A lower PMRI indicates caution: the training reference pool lacks close operational analogs for this cell, alerting operators to treat predictions with uncertainty or exercise abstention.
